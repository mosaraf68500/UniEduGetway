Our mission

Your background shouldn't decide your future.

Too many capable students never apply — because they assume they can't afford it, don't qualify for finance, or don't know where to start. We exist to remove that uncertainty: clear advice, honest eligibility checks, and hands-on support through every form and deadline.

We work with nine partner universities and colleges across London and beyond, and with students from every background — British nationals, EU citizens, refugees, and those with indefinite leave to remain.

Our story

From one adviser to a team of specialists.

2015

Founded in London

UniEdu Gateway starts as a single adviser helping local students with their university applications.

2018

Student finance support added
